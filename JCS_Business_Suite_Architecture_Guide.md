# JCS Business Suite
## Architecture Guide

Version: 1.0.0

Status:
Engineering Foundation

---

# Mission

JCS Business Suite is a modern, enterprise-grade, multi-business platform designed to allow businesses of every size to manage their operations from a single ecosystem.

The platform must operate consistently across:

• Apple
• Windows
• Android
• Web

while preserving a single business logic.

---

# Vision

Build one business platform.

Run everywhere.

Maintain one architecture.

---

# Engineering Principles

## Principle 1

Business Logic First

Business rules must never depend on the platform.

SwiftUI

React

Windows

Android

must all execute the same business rules.

---

## Principle 2

One Source of Truth

Every business entity must have one authoritative source.

Examples

Business

Customer

Employee

Inventory

Orders

Payments

Products

Services

Appointments

---

## Principle 3

Reusable Components

A component should be built once.

Then reused everywhere.

Never duplicate UI unnecessarily.

---

## Principle 4

Platform Consistency

The user experience should feel familiar regardless of device.

Mac

iPad

iPhone

Android

Windows

Browser

---

## Principle 5

Scalable Architecture

Every new feature should integrate into the architecture without requiring redesign.
